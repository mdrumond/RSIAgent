When a launcher caches launch plumbing, keep each launch on the stream the caller's current stream selects at call time, or establish ordering yourself. A cached stream handle can belong to an earlier call while inputs and consumers live on the current stream. Records: a creation-time-stream launch yields repeat results differing from an independent reference, while re-resolving or synchronising shows none; zero-initialising the output is no substitute. Synchronising is diagnostic, not a proven repair, and proves nothing about the asynchronous path. Validate with freshly produced inputs and a changed current stream, comparing first and repeated results separately. Only the currently selected stream and its synchronisation are attested spellings. Do not claim any speedup.
