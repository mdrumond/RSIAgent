Before any numerical repair, hoist both synchronization handles above the vector region by binding the results of tla.flag: loaded = tla.flag("ready", tla.arch.MTE2, tla.arch.VECTOR) and finished = tla.flag("done", tla.arch.VECTOR, tla.arch.MTE3), then stage through UB with tla.make_tensor_like(tla.allocate(LENGTH, tla.Float32, tla.AddressSpace.ub, 256), source, tla.arch.RowMajor) and the matching dest tensor, and inside with tla.vector() perform all four operations on those bound handles in dependency order: tla.copy(a, source) -> tla.set_flag(loaded) -> tla.wait_flag(loaded) -> the tla.vec.func(mode="simd") tile loop reading a and writing b -> tla.set_flag(finished) -> tla.wait_flag(finished) -> tla.copy(dest, b) -> tla.pipe_barrier(tla.pipes.ALL). The hazard is that constructing a flag without retaining and acting on it, or calling tla.tile_view/tla.load directly on the GM-space source and dest, either fails the lowering with an address-space error or silently produces tiled output that no longer matches the golden elementwise result; a pipe barrier alone does not substitute for the per-handle set/wait pairs, since barrier-only variants still diverge while variants with set/wait on both handles converge.
