For catlass-dsl SIMT chunked work-item kernels, make every work item's store span its whole chunk: after deriving the chunk base index, loop over the chunk's elements and store at base+offset so the store address advances with the work item's column index. A single store at the chunk base covers only 1/CHUNK of the output; that fraction is fixed by the work-item definition, so raising block_num or thread_block_dim cannot recover the missing elements. Diagnose with the coverage probe's work-item reason and simt_store_modes before device runs; treat the reported unreachable count and coverage_ratio as the share of the output the schedule cannot reach. Confirm the fix with a per-element write over each chunk.
