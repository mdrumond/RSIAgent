When a consumer addresses a tensor as a logical row over leading axes plus a trailing channel column, and the DLPack-style importer rejects the original high-rank source with a layout-metadata derivation error, adapt the input: pass a lower-rank view whose row extent is the product of the axes the consumer groups and whose column extent is the trailing axis, derived from the actual shape. Do not copy or clone; wrapped tensors must alias original storage. Use only keyword arguments the installed signature accepts, checking it rather than inventing stride or shape parameters; the layout tag is required. Choose the grouping from the consumer's indexing, since flattening all leading axes fits a consumer grouping every leading axis but not one expecting another pairing. Do not impose rejection rules on other ranks or extent values.
